How to play
  • Roll the dice to move around the board. The number you roll is quarters of your life passing — the ledger charges you for every one: paycheck, taxes, rent or mortgage, bills, her spending, kids, alimony, and debt payments with interest.
  • Where you land decides your fate: Payday, Choice and Chance squares deal cards with 5+ options; Debt, Rent and Trap squares just take your money, like real life.
  • Every choice then rolls a Fate — it can go as planned, break lucky, or backfire horribly. No choice is safe.
  • Use one free move before each roll: overtime, the boys, a doctor, the gym, or paying down debt.
  • Big birthdays unlock new life options. Stress rots your health, debt rots your credit — and the game only ends when you die. Your tombstone delivers the summary.

A parody of modern manhood. Any resemblance to your actual life is entirely your own fault.
