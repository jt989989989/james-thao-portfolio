      HOW EACH READ IS ANSWERED

      • Multiple choice — any subject: the answer shows as a letter plus the answer text, with a one-line reason — and the correct choice is highlighted green in the list of options under the answer. With Auto-scan on, a steady page is solved on its own; otherwise tap Capture & Read once and the answer appears as soon as the read finishes. Math is worked out, vocabulary is checked against a dictionary, and general-knowledge questions (science, geography, history and more) are checked against a built-in fact bank and then a reference lookup that scores each printed option. If two options could fit, it flags that instead of guessing.
      • Cybersecurity: a dedicated bank covers Security+ style questions — CIA triad, phishing / social engineering, malware types, firewalls, encryption and hashing, common ports, and incident response — and CISSP-domain questions across all 8 domains: security & risk management, asset security, security architecture & engineering, communication & network security, identity & access management, security assessment & testing, security operations, and software development security. Questions that name a term and offer definitions are scored against the bank's definition for that term.
      • Open questions: math shows the worked result; a general question shows the fact-bank answer, or the best reference match when there is one.
      • Open math: the worked result appears, with the expression it was calculated from.
      • Tap any word to define it: every scan reads the whole frame at once and puts a box on every word it finds — on the camera view and as chips in the Words in Frame panel. Tap any box or chip and its one-line definition appears instantly. No centering, no holding, no typing the word into a search box.
      • About this prototype: “Give a tough job to a lazy person and they’ll find the easiest way to solve it.” This tool is meant to do exactly that — you point, it reads and solves. Still a prototype, so expect the occasional misread.
      • Misreads happen: if the read confidence is low, check the text box — fix any wrong characters and tap Solve again.
