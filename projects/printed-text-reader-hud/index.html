Reading happens on this device — the camera image is never uploaded. Only the text inside the green frame is read: fit each question with all of its A–D choices inside it (move back a little rather than in close, so the choices fit too). Pointing at another screen? If the first read comes back unclear, the scan automatically re-reads the frame with a screen-cleanup pass that flattens glow and screen texture — hold steady and fill the frame. Every answer below is one of that question's own printed choices. Questions asked in words are solved too ("What is twelve divided by three?", "Mia shares 24 stickers equally among 4 friends") — and a story problem is answered even when its choices aren't in the frame ("A factory packs 480 juice boxes into packs of 24", "8 shelves, each holds 45 books"), with the worked answer shown right on the camera. The card highlights the question it read, and for word questions it marks the words it turned into a calculation and shows what it read them as. And because a ÷ can scan as a + when its dots are lost in screen glow, any + in a maths question is double-checked against the picture itself before it is trusted. Each question is anchored on its printed number ("2."), so that number is never treated as part of the maths, and anything read before it — the quiz title, the "4/5" progress marker — is ignored. If a question is read but its choices aren't in the frame, you still get a suggested answer worked from the question alone, labelled as a suggestion. And if the worked answer doesn't match any of that question's choices, the symbols and digits are re-analysed against the choices before anything is reported — a ÷ that read as + and a ? that read as an extra 7 are both retried the way the choices support (432 + 127 is re-read as 432 ÷ 12 when 36 is one of the choices). If the question and its choices still don't agree, the scan re-reads the frame with different treatments — a hard black-and-white pass, then flipped colours — and keeps whichever read makes them agree. So the answer the choices point to is the one you see, highlighted on the camera with the working that picked it shown on the highlighted choice.
