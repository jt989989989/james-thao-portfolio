How to play: Move your finger / mouse (or ← →) to aim the hovering blob, then tap the canvas, press Space, or hit DROP. Blobs are squishy — they deform, lean, and slide where they land, so aim for the center of balance, not just the top. Random wind gusts push harder the higher your tower gets. If the tower starts to go, time slows down… enjoy the fall. 🫠
Game over when a blob tumbles off the pedestal. Score = blobs still standing in your tower.
